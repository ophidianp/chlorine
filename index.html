Good to know

A few quick answers.

Does Chlorine change render distance?

No. Its adaptive tuning works with simulation distance; render distance is intentionally left alone to avoid expensive chunk render graph rebuilds.

Do I need another performance mod?

For Chlorine 1.0.3+ on Minecraft 26.3, Sodium 0.9.2 or newer is required because the workload governor integrates with Sodium's renderer. Lithium, FerriteCore, EntityCulling, ImmediatelyFast, and Iris remain optional.

Will it improve performance in every world?

Results depend on your hardware, world, entity count, and mod setup. Chlorine provides configurable tools, not a universal FPS guarantee.
