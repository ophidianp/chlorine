HOW TO READ THIS

Promising signal.
Not a final verdict.

Chlorine with performance mods had the highest sampled average: 531.6 FPS, about 63% above the Vanilla sample average. That result includes a 1,162 FPS reading from a particularly light scene, so it needs a controlled retest.

Only Chlorine averaged 333.6 FPS across its readable samples. These are sampled readings from the recordings, not a full frame-time analysis, so they don't establish that the other mods made performance slower.

ABOUT LOWS

Observed minimums
aren't 1% lows.

A true 1% low requires frame-time data across the full run. A handful of visible FPS-counter readings cannot show frame pacing or reliably calculate 1% lows.
