HOW TO READ THIS

Promising signal.
Not a final verdict.

Only Chlorine had the highest sampled average: 531.6 FPS, about 63% above the Vanilla sample average. That result includes a 1,162 FPS reading from a particularly light scene, so it needs a controlled retest.

The performance-mod recording averaged 278.6 FPS in its readable non-menu samples. Adding the requested estimated 55 FPS gives an adjusted average of 333.6 FPS. This is a contextual estimate—not a remeasurement—and does not establish that the other mods made performance slower.

ABOUT LOWS

Observed minimums
aren't 1% lows.

A true 1% low requires frame-time data across the full run. A handful of visible FPS-counter readings cannot show frame pacing or reliably calculate 1% lows.
