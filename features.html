WHAT CHLORINE DOES

Small adjustments.
A more considered world.

Chlorine focuses on CPU-side simulation, bursts of effects, and power use. It does not replace your renderer or change render distance.

01

CLIENT TUNING

Adapt to the moment.

The adaptive scaler watches a rolling FPS average, lowering simulation distance when performance dips and restoring it after a longer recovery cooldown. Optional fallbacks can adjust entity distance and particles.

A simulation-distance cap and fast-travel chunk governor give you more control. Chlorine changes simulation distance—not render distance—so it avoids rebuilding the chunk render graph.

02

WORLD SIMULATION

Ease background work.

Distant classic-mob and Brain-driven mob AI can be staggered when players are not nearby. Nearby mobs and physics are left alone.

Item and XP orb merging reduce the number of nearby entities. XP merging verifies value updates before discarding an orb and fails safe if it cannot.

03

EFFECTS & POWER

Make busy moments gentler.

Optional sound and particle budgets smooth bursts from farms, combat, fireworks, and other events. Quiet sounds, distant beacon beams, and portal particles can be pre-culled or throttled.

When Minecraft is unfocused or minimized, the power saver can cap FPS and hide clouds. Low-end auto-tune can apply lighter visual defaults once per launch.

04

YOUR CONFIG

Choose what stays on.

Features can be disabled individually. Configure them in-game with Cloth Config, optionally surfaced through Mod Menu, or edit config/chlorine.json directly.

Chlorine is designed to coexist with Sodium, Lithium, FerriteCore, EntityCulling, ImmediatelyFast, and Iris. Those mods are not required.

A NOTE ON PERFORMANCE

Results depend on your world.

Entity count, hardware, and other installed mods all matter. Defaults are conservative, not universal benchmarks. Throttling distant AI intentionally trades some background simulation frequency for lower CPU usage.
